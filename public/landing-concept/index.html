COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions & answers

Every business works differently. We can talk through the details together.

↗Ask us about your business
FAQ
01Will the content sound like my business?

We start with your brand voice, services, audience and approved facts. Drafts are checked against that context before they are used.

02Do I have to hand over my social passwords?

The intended setup uses each platform's authorised account connection. Access and supported actions depend on the platform and are confirmed during onboarding.

03Can you use my photos and videos?

Yes, when you provide them and confirm their permitted use. Existing footage can guide the content plan and any agreed editing workflow.

04How will I know if it is working?

We review publication, engagement and the enquiries or bookings that can be verified. Missing or uncertain data is labelled clearly.
